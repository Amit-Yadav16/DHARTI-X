APMC Trader Non-Payment Statutory Recourse

Under the Maharashtra APMC Act, 1963, traders must settle payment for agricultural produce within 24 hours of sale.

Step 1: Written APMC Notice File complaint with Palghar / Dahanu / Wada APMC Secretary with sale bill (Patti).
Step 2: Appeal to DDR If unresolved in 7 days, appeal to District Deputy Registrar (DDR) Cooperative Societies.
Step 3: License Attachment DDR holds authority to encash trader bank guarantees and cancel trading licenses.
